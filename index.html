User Study

In a paired study, 59 professionals compared videos generated from the same prompt and chose a preferred video or a tie. At least three experts evaluated each pair. The charts report wins, ties, and losses from the perspective of MovieRoll-Distilled against each baseline.

Method

A base-to-distilled framework combining streaming fine-tuning, long-rollout distillation, and long-horizon inference.

Benchmark Performance

We evaluate single-event generation at 10s, 30s, and 60s, and multi-event generation at 30s and 60s. HeliosBench rates visual quality, dynamics, smoothness, semantic alignment, and naturalness, together with drift over time. MovieRoll-Distilled obtains the highest total score among the evaluated methods in each reported setting. The table retains all method rows and individual ratings so that the overall score can be read alongside the underlying dimensions. Select a task and duration to inspect its results.

Higher ratings are better, including drift ratings. Total is the weighted sum. Single-event and multi-event evaluation use 200 and 100 prompts, respectively. MovieRoll-Base is not evaluated at 60 seconds. See the paper for the full protocol.
